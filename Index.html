Our Mission

Every Change Matters

PEI seeks to empower diverse women in Uganda by removing economic barriers, supporting women-led grassroots organizations, fostering sustainable businesses, and promoting financial independence. We strive to create an inclusive environment for all women to thrive.

1. Promoting Gender Equality:
One of our core objectives is to eliminate gender-based discrimination. Women and girls in many societies still face barriers in education, employment, and leadership roles. We provide scholarships, mentorship programs, and advocate for equal opportunities.

2. Economic Empowerment:
We offer vocational training, entrepreneurship programs, and support women-led businesses through microloans and financial literacy programs to bridge economic disparities.

About PEI

The Parity Empowerment Initiative (PEI) was founded in 2015 with the mission to create equal opportunities for women in Uganda. What began as a small community project has grown into a nationwide movement impacting thousands of lives annually.

Our Vision: A world where every woman has equal access to education, economic opportunities, and leadership positions regardless of her background.

Our Approach: We combine grassroots activism with systemic change, working directly with communities while advocating for policy reforms at national levels.

Impact to Date:
- 5,000+ women trained in vocational skills
- 1,200+ microloans disbursed
- 300+ girls receiving scholarships
- 50+ women-led businesses supported
